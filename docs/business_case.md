# Business Case - Customer Analytics Data Mart

## Problem

In most transactional systems, sales data is stored at the order or transaction level.

This creates a major limitation for business users:

- They cannot easily understand customer behavior
- Reporting requires complex SQL queries
- Customer insights are scattered across multiple queries
- No unified customer view exists

As a result, decision-making becomes slow and inconsistent.

---

## Solution

To solve this, I build a **Customer Analytics Data Mart**.

This data mart consolidates transactional sales data into a **single customer-centric analytical layer**.

It transforms raw fact table into business-ready customer metrics such as:

- Revenue KPIs
- Behavioral KPIs
- Product affinity metrics
- Customer segmentation
- Customer health score

---

## Business Value

This data mart enables:

- Faster BI reporting (Power BI  ready dataset)
- Customer segmentation for marketing campaigns
- Customer 360 view for analytics teams
- Executive dashboards for decision-making
- Customer health monitoring and churn risk detection

---

## Outcome

The final output is a scalable, automated SQL-based data mart that simulates a real-world Analytics Engineering pipeline.
